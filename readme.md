## About Me

I am a Marketing and Commercial Management professional with over 5 years of experience designing and executing growth strategies that connect data, technology, and business objectives.

My background combines strategic marketing, trade marketing, business intelligence, and commercial analytics, allowing me to transform complex data into actionable insights that improve decision-making, optimize operations, strengthen brand positioning, and drive sustainable business growth.
